INTRODUCING IFRIT

Automation for Mythical Beings.

Your personal
Ifrit.

Meet IFRIT (formerly Mythical Operator): a desktop app that automates your recurring activities in Mythical Beings. You choose the routines and set the limits. Your Ifrit handles the repetition, leaving you more time to play.

LESS REMEMBERING. MORE PLAYING.

The next cycle
won’t wait.

Many Mythical Beings activities follow a 3.5-day cycle. Life gets busy, a round slips by, and prizes go unclaimed or ingredients sit idle. Other activities, like Battlegrounds, need regular attention to keep your strategy moving.

IFRIT takes care of the repetition in the routines you configure, helping you keep a steady rhythm.

I

Full Collection Prizes

Automatically claim eligible prizes as collection cycles renew.

II

Incubation

Keep your incubation routine moving instead of leaving ingredients waiting.

III

Battlegrounds

Maintain a regular battle routine, following the strategy and limits you choose.

IV

Bounty

Keep track of recurring rounds. Bounty is observation-only in this beta.

YOUR ROUTINES. YOUR RULES.

An autopilot.
On your terms.

You decide which activities to automate and set the limits before they run. IFRIT follows that configuration, and you can pause new activity whenever you need.

Your Ifrit works on your computer. Keep IFRIT open with an active, authorized session for routines to run.
